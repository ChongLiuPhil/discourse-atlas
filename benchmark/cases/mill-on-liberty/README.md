# Mill: *On Liberty*, Chapter II excerpt

This is the first non-synthetic benchmark case in Discourse Atlas.

- **Author:** John Stuart Mill
- **Work:** *On Liberty* (1859), Chapter II, “Of the Liberty of Thought and Discussion”
- **Source:** Project Gutenberg eBook #34901
- **Status:** public-domain source text
- **Purpose:** test alternative defensible segmentation and explicit split/merge alignment on a real philosophical argument.

`reference-a.json` separates the branch-framing paragraph from the first fallibility argument. `reference-b.json` treats those two paragraphs as one discourse unit. `alignment-a-b.json` records that difference explicitly. Once aligned, the two references agree on the objection/reply structure and its evidence.

The case is intentionally short. It is a regression fixture and alignment demonstration, not a claim to exhaust the structure of Mill's chapter.
